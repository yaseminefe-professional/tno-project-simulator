How the numbers work

Each month the model updates actual cost and progress for every project, then applies standard earned value rules.

EV = Budget × % complete
CPI = EV ÷ Actual cost
SPI = EV ÷ Planned value
EAC = Actual + (Budget − EV) ÷ CPI
Variance = Budget − EAC

Status rules. Action needed when CPI is below 0.90 or the forecast overrun is above 10%. Watch when CPI is below 0.97 or SPI below 0.90. Otherwise on track.

Why it matters. Spotting a low CPI in month three gives a project leader time to replan. Spotting it at the final invoice does not.
